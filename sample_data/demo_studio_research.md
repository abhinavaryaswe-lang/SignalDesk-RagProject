# Demo research: booking problems at neighborhood fitness studios

**Dataset notice:** This is fictional practice data created only to demonstrate SignalDesk. It is not real customer research and should not be used to make business or investment decisions.

## Study setup

For this demo, imagine 12 short interviews with owners or managers of independent yoga and pilates studios in Pune. The counts below are invented for the demo. Participants could select more than one problem, so the counts do not add up to 12.

## What participants described

- 9 of 12 said they manage at least part of their class bookings or waitlists through WhatsApp messages or spreadsheets.
- 8 of 12 said late cancellations make it difficult to offer open spots to someone on a waitlist.
- 7 of 12 said customers ask staff how many class-pack sessions they have left.
- 6 of 12 said they sometimes miss or answer prospective-customer messages late when the studio is busy.
- 5 of 12 said they spend time reconciling bookings across more than one tool.

## Example interview notes

**Participant A, studio owner:** “Someone cancels in the afternoon, but the person on the waitlist may not see my message until the class has started.”

**Participant B, studio manager:** “We use a spreadsheet for class packs. People ask how many sessions they have left, and I have to check it manually.”

**Participant C, studio owner:** “Most people message us first. If we reply the next day, they may have already booked somewhere else.”

**Participant D, studio manager:** “I would like booking and cancellation updates in one place. I don't want another complicated system.”

## Early pricing reactions

- 4 of 12 said they would consider a simple booking tool priced at ₹500–₹1,500 per month if it saved staff time.
- 5 of 12 said they would consider paying only if it helped fill more classes or retain customers.
- 3 of 12 said they would not add a paid tool to their current budget.

These are hypothetical interview responses, not a representative survey or validated willingness-to-pay estimate.

## Questions this demo can support

The notes can support a summary of reported booking and waitlist problems, possible feature ideas, and the limits of the early pricing reactions. They do not provide Pune market size, competitor pricing, or evidence that studio owners would actually buy a product.
